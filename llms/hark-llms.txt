# Hark

> Hark is an artificial intelligence lab founded in 2025 by Brett Adcock (founder of Figure AI and Archer Aviation), headquartered in San Jose, California. It is building agentic, multimodal foundation models paired with purpose-built AI-native consumer hardware, intended to act as a universal interface between a person and their digital life. Hark emerged from stealth in March 2026 and raised a Series A of more than $700 million at a $6 billion post-money valuation in May 2026.

As of 2026-08-04 Hark is pre-product. The platform is in closed beta behind a request-access form, and Hark publishes **no developer portal, no developer documentation, no public API, no SDKs, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no MCP server and no A2A agent card** on any host it operates. This file is generated by API Evangelist from the company's public marketing surface — it is not a Hark-published document.

Disambiguation: this is hark.com (the Brett Adcock AI lab). It is not hark.eco (energy and IoT monitoring, which does operate api.hark.eco) and not gethark.ai (AI decision-intelligence middleware). Those are unrelated companies sharing the name.
